# Sign in

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the sign-in page, showing **Email or Username**, **Password**, **Login**, the Microsoft account button and the links under it

You sign in to Yaquoti Workflow with your email address or username and your password. If your organization uses Microsoft accounts, you can sign in with that account instead.

## Sign in with your password

1. Enter your **Email or Username**.
2. Enter your **Password**.
3. Select **Login**.

You land on the [Home page](../requests/home.md). Customers land on the customer portal instead; see [Customer portal: submit a request](../requests/customer-portal-new-request.md).

> ⚠️ **Four wrong passwords in a row lock your account for 5 minutes.** Wait, then try again, or reset your password.

If your account was added from your organization's directory, use your directory password. When the directory can't be reached, you can't sign in until it is back. See [Active Directory](active-directory.md).

## Sign in with a Microsoft account

Select the Microsoft account button under **OR**, and sign in in the window that opens.

The first time you sign in this way, you may be asked to fill in your work details. See [Complete your account](complete-your-data.md).

## Set your password the first time

If an administrator created your account, you're asked to replace your temporary password the first time you sign in.

1. Enter the new **Password**.
2. Enter it again in **Password (Confirm)**.
3. Select **Reset**.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Password** | 6 to 50 characters. At least one letter, one digit and one of `@ $ ! % * ? &`. Only letters, digits, those symbols and single spaces. |

You can't open any other page until the password is set.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your **Email address** and send the request.
3. Enter the **Verification Code (OTP)** you receive by email and select **Verify**.
4. Enter a new **Password** twice and select **Submit**.

The code is valid for 10 minutes. The new password follows the same rules as above.
