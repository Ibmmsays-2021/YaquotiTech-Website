# Create an account

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the **Sign Up** page with **Select User Type** showing **Customer** and **Vendor**

Customers and vendors can create their own account from the sign-in page. Employees don't sign up: an administrator creates their account (see [Users](users.md)), or they sign in with a Microsoft account (see [Sign in](sign-in.md)).

To open it, select **Sign Up** on the sign-in page.

## Sign up as a customer

A customer account lets you raise and follow requests through the customer portal.

1. Under **Select User Type**, select **Customer**.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email Address** and mobile number.
3. Optionally, add a phone number.
4. Accept the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**.
5. Select **Create your free account**.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Email Address** | Required. 5 to 50 characters. Must not already be used by any account. |
| Mobile | Required. Digits only. Must not already be used by any account. |

Your password is sent to your email address. Use it to [sign in](sign-in.md), then go to [Customer portal: submit a request](../requests/customer-portal-new-request.md).

## Sign up as a vendor

A vendor registration asks your organization to accept you as a supplier. The form has three steps.

1. Under **Select User Type**, select **Vendor**.
2. Fill in the contact information and select **Next**.
3. Fill in the company information and select **Next**.
4. Choose one of the **Categories** and fill in the form it shows.
5. Select **Submit**.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Required. 2 to 40 characters. |
| **Email Address** | Required. Must not already be used by any account. |
| Mobile | Required. Must not already be used by any account. |
| **Contact Person** | Optional. 3 to 50 characters. |
| **Description** | Required. 3 to 200 characters. |
| **Country**, **Currency** | Required. |
| **Web URL**, **Phone** | Optional. Must be a valid address and number. |

An administrator reviews your registration and replies by email. See [Vendors](vendors.md).

If the system can't send email at the moment, vendor registration is refused. Try again later, or contact the organization.
