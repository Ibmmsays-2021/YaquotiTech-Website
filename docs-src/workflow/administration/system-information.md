# Licence and version

> 📷 Screenshot needed: **Configuration** > **System Information** > **License**, showing **Client Information**, **License Term** and **Product Remain Usage**

The licence decides how long your organization can use Yaquoti Workflow and how much of it: for example, how many processes and categories can be active at once. **About** shows which version you are running, which support teams may ask for.

To view the licence, your system role needs **License** > **View**; to upload a new one, **Import**. See [System roles](system-roles.md).

Both pages are under **Configuration** > **System Information**: **License** and **About**.

## Check your licence

**License** shows:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Client Information** | Who the licence was issued to. |
| **Product Information** | What the licence covers. |
| **License Term** | When it starts and ends. |
| **Product Remain Usage** | For each limit, how much is used out of what is allowed. |

When a limit is reached, you can't activate another item of that kind until you deactivate one or renew the licence.

## Upload a new licence

1. Open **License**.
2. Under **Upload License File**, drag the file in or select **Choose a file**.
3. Select **Upload**.

The licence file is an XML file supplied by Yaquoti.

> ⚠️ **When the licence expires, every process is put on hold and approval groups are deactivated.** Requesters can't raise new requests until the licence is renewed. After uploading the new licence, release the processes and activate the groups again. See [Hold, release, move or copy a process](../process-design/process-lifecycle.md).

Uploading a new licence may not release them by itself. [TO VERIFY: whether a new licence releases held processes and approval groups]

## See the version

Open **About**. The **Current Build Number** is the version to quote when you contact support.
