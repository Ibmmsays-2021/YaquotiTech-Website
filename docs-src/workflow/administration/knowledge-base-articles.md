# Knowledge base

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the **Knowledge-Base** page, showing category cards, and one article open beside its **Article Information**

The knowledge base holds your organization's help articles: how-to guides, policies and answers to common questions. Use it to find an answer yourself before raising a request.

To read it, your system role needs the **Knowledge Base** permissions. See [System roles](system-roles.md).

To open it, select **Knowledge-Base** in the main menu.

## Categories

The knowledge base opens on its categories. Each card shows the category's name, description and status. Select **Select** on a card to see its articles.

## Articles

Inside a category, each article card shows its title and status. Select **Select** on a card to read the article. **Article Information** beside it shows the category, who wrote it and when.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Ready for everyone to read. |
| **Private** | Marked as internal. |
| **Draft** | Still being written. |

> ℹ️ **The status is a label only.** Everyone who can open the knowledge base sees every article in it, whatever its status.

To write or change articles, see [Manage the knowledge base](knowledge-base-management.md).
