# Built-in reports

> 📷 Screenshot needed: **Configuration** > **Reporting** > **Built-in Reports**, **Dashboard** tab, showing the request counts and comparison charts

Built-in reports are ready-made views of the requests in the system: how many there are, where they stand, and who is handling them. Use them for a quick answer; for anything they don't cover, build your own in the [Report builder](report-builder.md).

To open them, your system role needs the **Reports** permissions, and **Reports** > **Export** to export. Go to **Configuration** > **Reporting** > **Built-in Reports**.

## The reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Totals of in-progress, completed, escalated and cancelled requests, by day, week or month, with comparison charts and counts by process, administrator, approver and requester. |
| **Flash Report** | Requests filtered by **Status**, **Administrated By**, **Approved By** and a **From** / **To** date. |
| **All Requests** | Every request. |
| **By Request Code** | One request, found by its code. |
| **By Duration** | Requests raised **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. |
| **By Status** | **Completed**, **In Progress** or **Escalated** requests. |
| **Cancelled** | Requests cancelled **By Requester** or **During Process**. |
| **By Category** | Requests **By Process**, **By Administrator**, **By Approver** or **By Requester**. |

Request statuses are explained in [Request and step statuses](../concepts/statuses.md).

## Open or export the results

In any list, select **View** on a row to open the request's report. Select **Export** to download the list.
