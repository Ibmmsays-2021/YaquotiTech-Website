# Complete your account

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the **Account Setup** dialog, **Fill your Data**, with the fields filled in

The first time you sign in with a Microsoft account, Yaquoti Workflow may not know where you work yet. It asks you for your work details once, so you can be found by groups and approvers. You can also check that a printed request document is genuine without signing in.

## Fill in your work details

The **Account Setup** dialog opens by itself after you sign in.

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**.
2. Choose your **Region**, **Site** and **Department**.
3. Choose who you **Reports To**.
4. Choose your **Position** and position level, and enter your mobile number.
5. Select **Submit**, then **Finish**.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **First Name**, **Last Name** | Required. 2 to 20 characters, with no spaces at the start or end. |
| **Site** | Required. Lists the sites of the region you chose. |
| **Reports To** | Required. Can't be yourself. |

> ⚠️ **You can't use the system until an administrator accepts you.** Submitting sends your details to the administrators and your account stays inactive until one of them accepts it. You receive an email when your details are sent.

Administrators see accounts waiting for acceptance on the [Home page](../requests/home.md).

## Verify a printed request

A printed request carries a request code. Anyone can use it to confirm that the request exists in the system.

1. On the sign-in page, select **Verify Document**.
2. Enter the **Request Code** and select **Search**.

The page shows the request's ID, process, requester, creation date and due date. A request code is 15 to 35 characters long.
