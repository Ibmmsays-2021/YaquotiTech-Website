# Profile and settings

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the **Profile** page, **Profile** tab, showing the photo, the **Information** card and **My Workflows**

Your profile shows who you are in the organization: your role, department, manager and team. Settings let you change your password and the look of the system.

To open them, select your photo at the top of the page, then **Profile** or **Settings**.

## Your profile

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your system role, account, work and contact details, the processes you take part in (**My Workflows**) and **My Groups**. |
| **Team** | The people who report to you, and the other members of your department. |

Your details are kept up to date by administrators; you can't edit them here. To have one changed, contact your administrator. See [Users](users.md).

## Change your photo

1. On the **Profile** page, select **Edit** on the photo.
2. Upload a new photo, or remove the current one.

The photo can be up to 1 MB.

## Change your password

1. Go to **Settings** > **Security**.
2. Enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter the **New password** twice.
4. Select **Update**.

The new password must differ from the current one and follow the password rules in [Sign in](sign-in.md#set-your-password-the-first-time).

**Security** appears only for accounts created in Yaquoti Workflow. If you sign in with a Microsoft account or a directory account, change your password there. Customers reset their password from the sign-in page.

## Change the theme

Go to **Settings** > **Theme** and choose a colour theme and a **Theme Mode**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**.
