# Manage the knowledge base

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the **New Article** page, with a title, the article text in the editor and **Article Information** filled in

You keep the knowledge base up to date by organizing articles into categories and writing the articles themselves. See [Knowledge base](knowledge-base-articles.md) for how people read them.

To manage the knowledge base, your system role needs the **Knowledge Base** permissions: **Create**, **Update** and **Delete**. See [System roles](system-roles.md).

To open it, select **Knowledge-Base** in the main menu.

## Create a category

1. Select **New**.
2. Enter a **Category Name**, and optionally a **Category Description**.
3. Choose the **Category Status**.
4. Select **Submit**.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Category Name** | Required. 2 to 50 characters. Must be unique. |
| **Category Description** | Optional. Up to 100 characters. |

## Write an article

1. Open the category and select **New**.
2. Enter a **Title** and write the article in the editor.
3. Under **Article Information**, choose the **Article status**.
4. Select **Publish**.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Required. 3 to 50 characters. Must be unique across the whole knowledge base. |
| Article text | Required. 10 to 9,999 characters. |

**Publish** saves the article with the status you chose, including **Draft**. The status doesn't hide the article from readers.

## Edit a category or article

Open the card's menu and select **Edit**. For an article, select **Update** when you're done.

## Delete a category or article

1. Open the card's menu and select **Delete**.
2. Select **Submit** to confirm.

> ⚠️ **Deleting a category also hides all its articles, and can't be undone.** To remove only some articles, delete them one by one instead.
