# Customer portal: submit a request

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the customer portal's service details page, showing **Steps**, **Expected Completion Time** and **Fees**, with the **Start Service** button

The customer portal is where customers, people outside your organization, raise requests for the services you offer them and follow their progress. It has its own home page and menu: **Home**, **New Request**, **Inquiry** and **About Us**.

To use the portal, a customer needs a customer account. See [Create an account](../administration/sign-up.md).

Customers see only the processes marked as public, and only while they're valid and not held. See [Create a process](../process-design/process-basic.md).

## Portal home page

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Banner | Current announcements about services. See [Announcements](announcements.md). Select **Get Started** to browse the services, or **Track a request** to check one. |
| Service shortcuts | **Request Service** and **Inquiry**, plus **Request Feature** and **Submit Complaint** when your organization offers public processes with those exact names. |
| **Our Services** | Up to eight public services. Select **More Services** to see them all. |

## Submit a request

1. Select **New Request**, or **Get Started** on the home page.
2. Select a category, then a sub category, then the service. You can also search for any of them by name.
3. Read the service information, then select **Start Service**.
4. Fill in the form and submit it.

The service information shows the service's **Description**, its **Steps** with their duration, the **Expected Completion Time** and any **Fees**.

After you submit, the page shows **Your Request ID**. Keep it to track the request. Select **See the request** to open it, or **Back to home page**.

You're notified at each update the service is set up to send. To check where your request stands, see [Customer portal: track a request](customer-portal-inquiry.md).
