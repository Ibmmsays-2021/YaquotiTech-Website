# Home page

> 📷 Screenshot needed: the **Home** page, showing the welcome header with the profile picture, the date buttons and the request count cards

The home page is the first screen after you sign in. It shows how much work is waiting for you, a summary of requests, and your latest requests. Select **Home** in the top menu to come back to it.

## Your profile

The top of the page shows your name, picture, email and mobile number. To change your picture, select it and upload a new one, 1 MB at most. To change your other details, see [Profile and settings](../administration/profile-and-settings.md).

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** tab counts the requests you're involved in. Use the date buttons at the top to count all requests, or only those created today, in the last week or in the last month.

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Request count cards | How many requests are under **Pick**, **Assigned To You**, **Make An Action** and **From Escalation**. See [Requests list](requests-list.md). |
| **Overview** | More counts: escalated steps waiting in your queues, your closed requests, steps with open tasks, requests held for correction, and requests waiting for your update. |
| **Open Requests vs. Closed Requests** | A chart of all requests against closed requests, by process. |
| **Processes Number** | The number of processes your organization can use. |
| **Latest Requests** | Your three most recent requests, with priority and due date. Select one to open it. |

The cards only show figures. To work on the requests, open the matching filter in the [requests list](requests-list.md).

## Pending Vendors

If you administer a vendor category, the **Pending Vendors** tab lists vendors who registered in your category and are waiting for approval. The tab appears only while there is at least one.

1. Select **Registration Details** on a vendor to check their company and contact information.
2. Select **Accept**, or select **Reject** and explain the reason. The reason is sent to the vendor.

See [Vendors](../administration/vendors.md).
